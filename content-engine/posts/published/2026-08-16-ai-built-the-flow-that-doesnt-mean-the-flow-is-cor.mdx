## Variant A

You typed a prompt.

It gave you a welcome series.

Five emails, nicely spaced, discount in email three.

It gave you an abandoned cart flow too. Three touches, urgency in the last one.

Took you twenty minutes. Felt like a win.

Here's the problem.

That flow doesn't know your return window is 60 days, not 30.

It doesn't know your product gets reordered every 8 weeks, so sending a "we miss you" email at day 14 makes you look desperate.

It doesn't know your top spending segment should never see a discount, because they already pay full price and you're training them not to.

It doesn't know Christmas exists, or that your category dies in January, or that the customer who bought once in a sale is a different animal to the one who bought at full price.

AI can build a flow that looks correct.

Structurally, it's fine. Five emails, sensible gaps, a discount somewhere in the middle.

But "looks correct" and "is correct" are different things.

A flow that sends on schedule isn't the same as a flow that's actually configured for your business.

The gap between those two is usually invisible until you look at the revenue per email and wonder why it's flat.
