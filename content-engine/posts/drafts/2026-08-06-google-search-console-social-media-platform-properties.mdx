## Variant A

Your social content has been showing up in Google Search for years.

You've had no idea how it was performing.

That changed in July 2026.

Google Search Console added platform properties. You can now connect your Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube accounts directly, without needing a website, and see exactly how your content performs in Search and Discover.

Clicks. Impressions. Average position. Click-through rate.

And the one that actually matters: the queries people searched after finding your content.

That last report is worth paying attention to.

Because when someone sees your post and then searches Google for something related, they're telling you something your engagement metrics never will. They're not just scrolling. They're interested enough to go and look for more.

Those queries are intent data. Real intent. The kind you'd normally spend serious budget trying to uncover through keyword research.

And here's where most social marketers stop short.

They use the data to optimise their social content. Sensible move.

But the more valuable application is email.

If your posts about a certain topic are generating search traffic around specific questions, that's a list of exactly what your audience wants to understand more deeply. That's your email opt-in offer. That's your next nurture sequence.

Social gets someone interested. Search tells you what they're interested in. Email is where you actually do something with that interest.

The setup takes under five minutes per account. The data takes a few days to appear.
