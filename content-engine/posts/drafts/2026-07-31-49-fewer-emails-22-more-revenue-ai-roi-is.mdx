## Variant A

49% fewer emails. 22% more revenue.

That's what one brand achieved by moving AI out of copy generation and into lifecycle orchestration.

Braze published a guide this week on why most brands aren't seeing AI ROI. The three roadblocks they identify — over-promising tools, bad data, talent gaps — are all worth reading.

But the thread running through every case study is the same: the brands seeing results aren't using AI to draft email content faster. They're using it to make decisions a human team can't make at scale.

Who gets which message. When. Based on behavioural signals, resolved in real time.

Copy quality is worth maybe 10–20% of email performance. The rest is structural — timing, segmentation, trigger logic, sequencing. That's where AI actually moves revenue.

If your AI implementation is: brief → draft → edit → send, you're getting a productivity win. Not a performance win.

The direction worth moving: less volume, smarter decisioning, compounding returns.
