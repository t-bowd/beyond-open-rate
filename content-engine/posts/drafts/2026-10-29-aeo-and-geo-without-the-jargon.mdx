## Variant A

Someone said "AEO" in a meeting.

You nodded.

Later you typed it into a search bar.

Here's the plain version, no jargon.

AEO is Answer Engine Optimisation. It means making your content easy for AI tools to pull an answer from when someone asks a question.

GEO is Generative Engine Optimisation. It means showing up by name when an AI tool recommends who to use or buy from.

Neither one replaces SEO. They sit on top of it.

If your site is thin, slow or confusing to a search engine, it's just as confusing to an AI tool reading it on your behalf.

Three things worth doing before anything fancier:

Write content that answers specific questions directly, in plain sentences, near the top of the page. AI tools lift answers, not vibes.

Make sure your business name, location and what you actually do are stated clearly and consistently across your site and listings. AI tools recommend what they can confirm, not what they have to guess at.

Get mentioned on other credible sites, not just your own. AI tools weight what other people say about you, the same way word of mouth always has.

None of this is a trick. It's the same groundwork that's made SEO work for twenty years, pointed at a newer audience.

## Variant B

AEO. GEO. SEO. AI search. Nobody defines these in one sentence, so everyone just nods along.

Here they are, properly.

AEO is Answer Engine Optimisation. Making your content easy for an AI tool to lift an answer from.

GEO is Generative Engine Optimisation. Getting your business named when an AI tool makes a recommendation.

Both are built on SEO. Neither works without it.

If an AI tool can't crawl your site properly or can't tell what you actually sell, it can't answer with you and it definitely can't recommend you.

Three places to start, before you chase anything more advanced:

One. Answer real questions in plain language on your pages, not just keywords stuffed into headings. If a customer would ask it out loud, it should be answered in a sentence they could read aloud back.

Two. Keep your business details consistent everywhere. Name, location, what you do. AI tools cross-check before they recommend, same as a cautious customer would.

Three. Get named on other sites that already have credibility. Directories, press, partner pages, reviews. An AI tool trusts what other people say about you more than what you say about yourself.

Good SEO was never just for Google. It was always the groundwork. This is just the next place it's being read.
