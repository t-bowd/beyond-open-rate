Your customer isn't the same. Price point, purchase reason, average order value, all shifted. The flow still talks to the customer you had in 2023.

None of this shows up as a red flag on your dashboard. It just shows up as slowly declining revenue you can't quite explain.

Flows aren't a one-time setup. They're a living part of your business that ages the moment your product or customer does.
