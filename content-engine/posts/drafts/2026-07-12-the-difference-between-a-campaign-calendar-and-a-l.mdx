## Variant A

You have a content calendar full of email campaigns.

You're sending. You're consistent. You've got Black Friday mapped out three months in advance.

That's not lifecycle marketing.

That's broadcast marketing with better planning.

Here's what almost no one has: a system that sends the right email based on what the customer just did.

Someone abandons a cart. They get a recovery sequence.
Someone makes a second purchase. They enter a retention track.
Someone goes 90 days without buying. They get a winback flow before they churn.

Campaigns are top-down. You decide what to send and when.
Lifecycle is bottom-up. Customer behaviour triggers the message.

Both matter. But campaigns alone leave 60–70% of potential email revenue on the table.

Most brands doing $2–10M have 8–12 active campaigns per month and maybe 3 automated flows. It should be the opposite.

The maths is brutal. If you're driving traffic and acquiring customers but you don't have lifecycle automation responding to their behaviour, you're paying full CAC and collecting partial LTV.

## Variant B

Your campaigns are doing fine.

Open rates are decent. Click rates are average. Revenue trickles in when you send.

But here's what you're not capturing:

The customer who bought once 68 days ago and hasn't come back.
The one who added $240 to cart yesterday and ghosted.
The one who's opened your last four emails but never clicked.

You're not responding to any of that because your email strategy is a schedule, not a system.

A campaign calendar tells you what to send next Tuesday.
A lifecycle strategy tells you what to send when someone does something.

Purchase. Browse. Abandon. Engage. Ignore. Churn.

Every one of those behaviours is a revenue signal. Most brands only respond to one of them (abandoned cart), and even then it's usually a single generic email.

The gap between "we send regular campaigns" and "we have full lifecycle automation" is worth 40–60% more revenue from the same traffic.

Not from spending more. From responding faster and more precisely to what your customers are already doing.
