## Variant B

Thirty seconds.

That's how long it took the AI to write the email.

Twenty minutes.

That's how long it took to decide whether to send it.

Most founders think the second number is the problem. Get a better tool and it'll shrink.

It won't. Because those twenty minutes were never about writing.

It was the segment check. Is this landing with people who've actually engaged, or everyone who signed up back in 2022?

It was the suppression logic. Anyone mid-return, mid-dispute, mid-complaint gets pulled before this goes near their inbox.

It was the calendar check. Three other automations are already live. Does this one collide with them, or quietly cannibalise a flow that's already converting?

It was the brand voice pass. AI defaults to confident and generic. Someone has to make it sound like your brand, not every other brand using the same tool.

It was the lifecycle read. Where is this customer, actually? Day two feels nothing like day forty.

None of that shows up in a prompt window.

AI collapsed the writing time to almost nothing.

It left the decision layer exactly where it was.

That part was always the slow part. It still is.
