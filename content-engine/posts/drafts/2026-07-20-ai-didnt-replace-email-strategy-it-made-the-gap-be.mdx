## Variant A

You started using AI to write your emails.

Now you think your email program is sorted.

It's not.

AI can write the email in 30 seconds. That was never the hard part.

The hard part is deciding who gets it. When. Who gets suppressed. What context they're in when it lands.

That still takes judgment. AI doesn't have it.

Here's the part most founders miss.

AI gave every brand on earth the same production speed. Your competitor's AI writes just as fast as yours.

Which means speed is no longer an advantage. It's the baseline.

Strategy is the only thing left that separates you from them.

Brands with weak strategy just found a faster way to send weak emails.

## Variant B

Every ecommerce brand can now write an email in 30 seconds.

Yours. Your competitor's. The brand that's about to eat your market share.

Same speed. Same tools. Same output quality on the surface.

So what actually separates the brands winning right now from the ones losing revenue?

Not the writing.

The decisions underneath it.

Who receives this email and who gets suppressed. What sequence they're in. What happened right before this send. What happens if they don't open it.

That's strategy. AI doesn't do that. It never did.

For years, production speed was a real advantage. Brands with more resources could simply send more, faster.

That advantage is gone. AI erased it for everyone at once.

What's left is judgment. And judgment is harder to fake than a well written subject line.
