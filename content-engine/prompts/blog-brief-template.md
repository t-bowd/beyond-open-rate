Write a blog post using this brief:

TOPIC: [topic]
TARGET KEYWORDS: [target_keywords]
PRIMARY KEYWORD: [primary_keyword]
SEED CLAIM: [seed_claim]
AUDIENCE ANGLE: [audience_angle]
FORMAT: [format]
NOTES: [notes]

SEO REQUIREMENTS
The primary keyword must appear:
- Naturally in the first 100 words
- In at least one H2 heading
- In the meta description (added via the METADATA block)

Secondary keywords should appear naturally throughout — do not force them.

QUALITY BAR
This post should be the most useful thing someone searching for "[primary_keyword]" has ever read on this topic. Specific numbers, thresholds, and examples throughout. No filler. No obvious points stated as insights.
