If you Google "Klaviyo agency Australia" right now you'll get a wall of near-identical landing pages, all claiming to be "certified experts" with "proven results." Most of them are telling the truth in the loosest possible sense. Certification tiers are real, but they measure something narrower than most agencies let on, and knowing what they actually signal is the difference between hiring a specialist and hiring someone who set up their Klaviyo account eighteen months ago and never looked back.

This guide breaks down what the tiers mean, how to verify them, what Australian pricing actually looks like, and the questions that separate a genuine Klaviyo partner from a generic marketing agency with a Klaviyo login.

## What the Certification Tiers Actually Measure

Klaviyo runs a partner programme with four tiers: **Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum**. The tiers aren't a skills exam. They're based primarily on two things: how much revenue Klaviyo can attribute to email and SMS sent through an agency's managed client accounts, and how many active clients the agency runs on the platform.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. An agency can hold Gold status because it manages twenty small ecommerce stores that collectively push a lot of revenue through Klaviyo, without any single account manager having deep flow strategy experience. Tier tells you scale, not necessarily skill on your specific account.

Here's roughly what each tier represents:

- **Bronze**: Entry-level partner status. Usually smaller agencies or freelancers early in their Klaviyo client base.
- **Silver**: A working track record. Multiple active clients, consistent platform use, but not yet managing large revenue volumes.
- **Gold**: Established partner. Significant revenue under management, usually a dedicated Klaviyo partner manager assigned to the agency.
- **Platinum**: Klaviyo's top tier. Reserved for agencies managing substantial revenue across a large client base, often with direct input into Klaviyo's product roadmap and beta features.

None of this tells you whether the agency understands your abandoned cart flow needs to be different for a $40 average order value store versus a $400 one, or whether a service business's nurture sequence should be built around booked calls instead of purchases. That's a separate conversation, covered below.

> **Benchmark:** Klaviyo's own partner directory lists partner tier and location, and agencies cannot self-report a higher tier than what Klaviyo has assigned. If an agency's website badge doesn't match their directory listing, that's your answer.

## Comparing Service Offerings by Tier

| Capability | Bronze / Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow build & optimisation | Usually yes, basic templates | Yes, custom flow architecture | Yes, advanced segmentation and predictive analytics |
| Direct Klaviyo support access | Limited | Dedicated partner manager | Priority access, beta features |
| SMS strategy | Sometimes | Usually included | Usually included with deliverability consulting |
| Migration support (from Mailchimp, Klaviyo re-platform) | Basic | Full-service | Full-service with data audit |
| Reporting depth | Standard dashboards | Custom revenue attribution reporting | Custom attribution plus lifecycle modelling |
| Best fit for | Small stores, tight budgets | Mid-size ecommerce, established service businesses | High-volume ecommerce, multi-brand operators |

Tier is a useful filter, not a final answer. A well-run Silver partner that specialises in service businesses will outperform a Platinum agency that's never built a lead-nurture sequence for a consultant selling $15,000 engagements.

## Verifying Credentials Before You Sign Anything

Do these three things before any contract:

1. **Ask for their live partner directory link.** Not a screenshot. A URL you can click.
2. **Ask which of their current clients are in your vertical.** An agency that's only ever worked with fashion ecommerce stores will build the wrong thing for a bookkeeping firm trying to nurture EOFY leads.
3. **Ask to see one flow performance report from an existing client** (anonymised is fine). If they can't produce one, they haven't been managing accounts long enough to have the data.

If you want a faster gut check before you even get on a call with an agency, [run the free email program audit](/tools/email-audit) on your own account first. It'll tell you where your current setup stands, so you walk into any pitch already knowing what needs fixing rather than taking an agency's word for it.

## What Australian Pricing Actually Looks Like

Pricing in the Australian market varies more by scope than by agency size. A few benchmarks:

- **Setup/migration projects**: $2,000 to $8,000, depending on list size and number of flows being rebuilt.
- **Ongoing retainers for ecommerce**: $1,500 to $3,000/month for stores under $2M revenue, scaling to $4,000 to $6,000+ for larger operations with segmented campaigns and SMS.
- **Ongoing retainers for service businesses**: Often lower volume but higher strategic complexity. Expect $1,500 to $3,500/month for a nurture and retention programme built around booked calls rather than purchases.

> **Benchmark:** Agencies charging under $1,000/month for full-service management are almost always running templated flows with minimal customisation. That's fine for a brand-new store with no data yet. It's a poor fit for anyone with an established list they're not currently monetising.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring

For **ecommerce founders**, ask: "How do you handle abandoned cart timing differently for high-AOV versus low-AOV stores?" and "What's your typical lift in recovered cart revenue in the first 90 days?" A specific answer with a number beats a vague "we optimise everything."

For **service business operators**, ask: "How do you structure a nurture sequence for a buyer who takes three months to decide?" and "Have you built flows tied to booked calls rather than checkout events?" Most Klaviyo agencies live and breathe ecommerce. Fewer have built lead-to-call sequences for consultants, agencies, or SaaS trials, and that's a genuinely different skill set inside the same platform.

## The Tier Isn't the Decision, the Fit Is

Certification tells you an agency has scale and a working relationship with Klaviyo's platform team. It doesn't tell you they understand your margin structure, your sales cycle, or why your unsubscribe rate spiked after your last EOFY campaign. Ask for proof specific to your situation, check the directory listing yourself, and treat the badge as a starting filter rather than the final word.
