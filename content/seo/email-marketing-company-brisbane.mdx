## Email marketing in Brisbane: what's changed

Brisbane's email marketing landscape has shifted the same way it has across Australia: businesses are moving away from agencies that report on open rates and vanity metrics, toward partners who can show a direct line from email activity to recovered revenue. Beyond Open Rate is an Australian email lifecycle agency specialising in this shift, working with Brisbane e-commerce brands and service businesses that want their email channel treated as a revenue line, not a marketing checkbox.

The agency works across Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot, Omnisend, and Brevo, with no referral relationship to any of them. That matters in a market like Brisbane, where a lot of local agencies default to whichever platform pays them the highest commission, regardless of fit.

## Why Brisbane businesses are switching agencies

Two patterns come up repeatedly when Brisbane businesses reach out to Beyond Open Rate. The first is e-commerce brands on Shopify or BigCommerce who have Klaviyo installed but are only running a handful of default flows, leaving abandoned cart and post-purchase sequences largely untouched. The second is service businesses, agencies, and SaaS companies with an email list sitting idle in a CRM, sending occasional newsletters with no lifecycle structure behind them.

> **Revenue benchmark:** Well-built e-commerce email flows typically drive 15-25% of total store revenue; most Brisbane brands Beyond Open Rate audits are sitting at under 8%.

Service businesses see a different pattern: leads that come through email nurture sequences convert at meaningfully higher rates than cold outbound, but only when the sequence is built around buying triggers rather than generic drip content.

## The audit-first approach

Beyond Open Rate doesn't start with a rebuild. Every engagement begins with an audit of the current setup, covering deliverability, flow performance, segmentation, and list health. For Brisbane businesses this usually surfaces the same handful of issues: sending domains without proper authentication, welcome series that stop after one email, and segments based on demographics instead of purchase or engagement behaviour.

> **Deliverability benchmark:** Businesses without SPF, DKIM, and DMARC properly configured typically see 10-15% of email land in spam or promotions folders, silently cutting into revenue before a campaign even sends.

The audit produces a prioritised list of fixes ranked by expected revenue impact, so a Brisbane business knows exactly what to fix first and what it's worth, before agreeing to any ongoing retainer.
