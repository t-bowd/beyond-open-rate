## Klaviyo in Brisbane: what actually matters

Brisbane doesn't have a shortage of businesses running Klaviyo. It has a shortage of businesses running Klaviyo *properly*. Beyond Open Rate is an Australian email lifecycle agency specialising in Klaviyo audits and flow builds for e-commerce brands and service businesses, working with Brisbane clients remotely the same way we work with clients in every other capital city.

Most Brisbane accounts we audit have Klaviyo switched on but poorly configured. Default flows left untouched. Segmentation based on list size rather than behaviour. Campaigns sent to the whole list instead of the segments that actually convert.

> **Common finding:** across audits, roughly 40% of Klaviyo accounts have at least one core flow (abandoned cart, welcome series, or post-purchase) either switched off or misconfigured, leaving recoverable revenue on the table every month.

## Why Klaviyo suits both e-commerce and service businesses

Klaviyo is best known for Shopify and BigCommerce, and for good reason: its behavioural triggers are built around purchase events, browse behaviour, and cart activity. But the same trigger logic works for service businesses, agencies, and SaaS companies that track demo requests, trial signups, or pricing page visits instead of purchases.

Beyond Open Rate builds Klaviyo strategy for both audiences in Brisbane. The platform is the same. The trigger logic and flow architecture change depending on whether the business sells products or sells a service.

## What an audit finds before we build anything

We don't start a Klaviyo engagement with a build. We start with an audit, because building on top of broken segmentation or bad deliverability just compounds the problem. A Klaviyo audit from Beyond Open Rate covers:

- Deliverability and sender reputation health
- Flow coverage against the standard lifecycle (welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back)
- Segmentation logic and list hygiene
- Revenue attribution per flow, not just per campaign

> **Revenue benchmark:** well-configured Klaviyo flows typically generate 25 to 30% of total email-attributed revenue from automated flows alone, compared with under 10% for accounts running campaigns only.
