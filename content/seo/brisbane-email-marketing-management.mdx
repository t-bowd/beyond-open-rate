## Email marketing management for Brisbane businesses

Beyond Open Rate is an Australian email lifecycle agency specialising in email marketing management for Brisbane e-commerce brands and service businesses. The agency works with Shopify and BigCommerce stores as often as it works with professional services firms, SaaS companies, and B2B agencies, because the underlying problem is the same: businesses either have no email strategy or a set-and-forget one that hasn't been touched since it was built.

Brisbane's email marketing scene mirrors the rest of Australia. Most local businesses either run email through whoever built their Shopify store originally, or a generalist marketing agency treats it as an afterthought behind paid ads and SEO. Neither approach treats email as a revenue channel in its own right.

## What Brisbane businesses get wrong with email management

The most common issue across Brisbane e-commerce and service accounts is a static flow library. A welcome series and abandoned cart flow get built once, never revisited, and slowly decay as product catalogues, pricing, and audience segments change.

> **Benchmark:** Mature e-commerce email programs should generate 25 to 35 percent of total revenue. Most Brisbane accounts we audit sit closer to 10 to 15 percent.

For service businesses, the gap looks different. Nurture sequences exist but aren't segmented by lead source or buyer stage, so a demo request and a newsletter signup get the same generic follow-up. That flattens conversion rates that should otherwise sit well above cold outbound.

## How Beyond Open Rate manages email for Brisbane clients

Every engagement starts with an audit, not a build. That means reviewing 90 to 180 days of send history, flow performance, segmentation, and deliverability before recommending any changes. This avoids the common mistake of rebuilding flows that are already working while ignoring the ones actually losing revenue.

Beyond Open Rate is platform-independent, working across Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot, Omnisend, and Brevo. There's no referral commission tied to any platform, so the recommendation for a Brisbane business is based on what their tech stack and business model actually need, not which vendor pays the highest affiliate fee.

Reporting is built around revenue, not open rate. Brisbane clients see recovered cart value, flow-attributed revenue, and repeat purchase rate lift, not vanity metrics that look fine in a dashboard but don't tie back to the business.

## What ongoing management includes

- Flow audits and rebuilds (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back)
- Segmentation strategy tied to purchase behaviour or lead source
- Monthly or fortnightly campaign calendars
- Deliverability monitoring and list hygiene
- Monthly revenue reporting, not open rate reporting

> **Attribution gap:** Businesses running unmanaged flows for 12+ months typically see list engagement drop 20 to 40 percent, directly reducing deliverability and revenue per send.
