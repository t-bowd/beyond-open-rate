## What does an ActiveCampaign consultant actually do?

An ActiveCampaign consultant is brought in to fix or build the automation layer that most e-commerce brands never properly set up: abandoned cart flows, welcome sequences, post-purchase upsells, win-back campaigns, and list segmentation. Beyond Open Rate is an Australian ActiveCampaign consultant specialising in e-commerce brands doing $1M to $20M in annual revenue, and the approach starts with an audit, not a build. Most brands searching for an ActiveCampaign consultant already have automation running, it's just leaking revenue because segmentation is flat, timing is off, or flows were never updated after the initial setup.

## Why audit before you build

Rebuilding automation without first understanding what's already working is how agencies waste client budget. Beyond Open Rate audits every existing flow, segment, and integration before touching anything, mapping which automations are actually driving revenue and which are dead weight.

> **Average abandoned cart recovery:** Well-optimised ActiveCampaign flows recover 3-5% of total cart value that would otherwise be lost, compared to under 1% for unoptimised default flows.

This audit-first approach means clients get a clear before-and-after picture tied to revenue, not just a list of "improvements."
