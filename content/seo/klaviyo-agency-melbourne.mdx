Melbourne has one of the strongest e-commerce ecosystems in Australia — fashion, beauty, homewares, food, and specialty retail, with a high concentration of Shopify-native DTC brands that have outgrown their first email setup.

Most of them are on Klaviyo. Fewer are getting close to what their account is capable of.

The gap is almost always the same: a welcome series and an abandoned cart flow built at launch, campaigns going to the full list, and no clear view of what email actually contributes to revenue.

## What most Melbourne Klaviyo accounts are missing

**Browse abandonment.** Consistently underbuilt, consistently high-performing. When a subscriber browses a product or category and leaves without adding to cart, that's a clear buying signal. A well-timed browse abandonment flow brings a meaningful percentage back. Most accounts don't have it.

**Segmentation.** Sending the same campaign to your entire list is one of the fastest ways to damage deliverability and depress results. Engagement-based segments, purchase behaviour tiers, and product category splits make every send more relevant and protect your sender reputation over time.

**Post-purchase sequences.** The moment after purchase is when trust is highest. A good post-purchase flow includes a review request at the right time, a cross-sell based on what they bought, and a replenishment reminder for consumable products. Most accounts have an order confirmation and nothing else.

**Revenue reporting.** Klaviyo's default dashboard shows opens and clicks. What tells you whether the program is working is revenue per recipient, flow contribution to total revenue, and abandoned cart recovery rate. We build reporting that gives you that view.

## What we do

**Flow builds and optimisation.** We build or rebuild the core flows — welcome series, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase, win-back — with correct trigger logic, audience exclusions, and copy that converts. We test and tune them month over month.

**Campaign management.** A monthly calendar of broadcast campaigns: planned, written, designed, and sent to the right segments. We own the cadence so you're not thinking about what to send next month.

**Klaviyo migrations.** Full migrations from other platforms, including data export and cleaning, segment recreation, flow rebuilding, domain authentication, and a phased warm-up plan.

**Deliverability.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured correctly. List hygiene on a regular cadence. Sunset policies for long-inactive subscribers. Monitoring that catches problems before they affect your sending reputation.
