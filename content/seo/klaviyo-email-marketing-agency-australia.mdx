## What a proper Klaviyo engagement looks like

Most agencies start by rebuilding flows. We start by auditing what's already there, because rebuilding a broken segmentation strategy just produces a faster version of the same underperformance.

Our process:

1. **Audit** — flow-by-flow revenue attribution, segment logic, deliverability score, list growth rate
2. **Prioritise** — rank fixes by expected revenue impact, not effort required
3. **Build** — abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back and lead nurture flows rebuilt against the audit findings
4. **Report** — monthly reporting on recovered cart revenue, repeat purchase rate and LTV lift, not open rate

> **Flow revenue benchmark:** well-optimised Klaviyo flows generate 25 to 30% of total email-attributed revenue, with abandoned cart flows converting 3 to 5 times higher than standalone campaigns.

## Signs your Klaviyo account needs an audit

- Flow revenue sits below 15% of total email-attributed revenue
- Abandoned cart flows convert under 3%
- List growth has stalled for two or more months
- Segmentation is based on list membership rather than purchase or engagement behaviour
- Deliverability has dropped and nobody can explain why

> **Deliverability signal:** accounts with unmonitored sender reputation typically see a 10 to 20% drop in inbox placement within six months of being left unaudited.
