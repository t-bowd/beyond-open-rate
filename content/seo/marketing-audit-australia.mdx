## What a marketing audit in Australia should actually deliver

Beyond Open Rate is an Australian marketing audit specialising in email and lifecycle marketing performance for both e-commerce brands and service businesses. Most marketing audits stop at a checklist: is there a welcome flow, is there an abandoned cart sequence, is the sender domain authenticated. That's a starting point, not a result. A useful marketing audit ties every finding back to revenue, whether that's recovered cart revenue for a Shopify store or pipeline velocity for a B2B SaaS company.

> **Industry benchmark:** businesses without a properly configured abandoned cart flow leave an average of 18% of recoverable revenue unclaimed each month.

## Why "marketing audit Australia" searches often lead to generic checklists

Search this query and you'll mostly find agencies selling a free audit as a lead magnet for their own retainer. The audit becomes a sales pitch dressed as diagnostics. Beyond Open Rate runs audits as a standalone, paid engagement precisely to avoid this bias. The recommendation isn't "sign with us for twelve months," it's a prioritised list of fixes ranked by revenue impact, some of which a business can implement in-house.

## What's covered in a proper audit

- **Flow architecture** — welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, and for service businesses, lead nurture and re-engagement sequences
- **Segmentation logic** — whether lists are segmented by behaviour and value or just by acquisition source
- **Deliverability** — sender authentication, list hygiene, and inbox placement trends over the past 90 days
- **Platform fit** — whether Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot, Omnisend, or Brevo is actually the right tool for the business's data volume and complexity
- **Revenue attribution** — what percentage of total revenue is currently trackable back to email and lifecycle marketing

> **Attribution gap:** businesses that haven't had an audit in over 12 months typically misattribute 20-30% of email-driven revenue to other channels due to broken tracking.

## Service businesses need this too

Marketing audits get framed as an e-commerce exercise because cart recovery is easy to quantify. But professional services firms, agencies, and B2B SaaS companies lose just as much revenue to broken lead nurture, inconsistent follow-up cadence, and CRM data that never gets used for segmentation. Beyond Open Rate audits HubSpot and ActiveCampaign instances for service businesses with the same rigour applied to Klaviyo audits for e-commerce clients, because the underlying question is the same: is this system converting contacts into revenue at the rate it should be.
