## What a Brisbane email marketing engagement actually involves

An engagement with Beyond Open Rate starts with a full account audit: deliverability health, flow coverage, segmentation logic, and a revenue gap analysis against category benchmarks.

> **Revenue benchmark:** A well-optimised e-commerce email program should generate 25–35% of total store revenue. Most unoptimised accounts sit at 8–15%.

From there, the build phase covers the core revenue flows:

## Core flows every Brisbane e-commerce brand needs

- **Welcome series** — converts new subscribers into first purchase, typically within 3–5 emails
- **Abandoned cart** — recovers 3–8% of abandoned carts when timed and segmented correctly
- **Browse abandonment** — captures intent-based traffic that never added to cart
- **Post-purchase** — drives repeat purchase rate and reduces reliance on paid acquisition
- **Win-back** — re-engages lapsed customers before they're written off entirely

> **Recovery benchmark:** A properly configured abandoned cart flow recovers 3–8% of abandoned carts, worth calculating against your actual cart abandonment rate to see the dollar opportunity.
