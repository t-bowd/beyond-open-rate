## Why Brisbane businesses need more than campaign sends

A lot of "email marketing" in Brisbane still means someone in the marketing team sending a weekly newsletter and calling it strategy. That's not lifecycle marketing. Proper email lifecycle work means:

- **Automated flows** that trigger on customer behaviour (cart abandonment, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back)
- **Segmentation** based on purchase history, engagement and lifecycle stage, not just demographics
- **Revenue reporting** tied to each flow and campaign, not just open and click rates

> **Flow versus campaign revenue split:** For most e-commerce brands, automated flows generate 60-70% of total email revenue despite representing less than 5% of total sends.

## E-commerce and service businesses need different builds

Brisbane's economy is a genuine mix of retail, e-commerce and professional services, and email strategy needs to reflect that. For e-commerce brands on Shopify or BigCommerce, the priority is cart and browse abandonment, post-purchase sequences, and win-back flows tied directly to repeat purchase rate. For service businesses, SaaS companies and B2B firms, the priority shifts to lead nurture sequences, onboarding flows, and re-engagement campaigns tied to pipeline and customer lifetime value.

Beyond Open Rate builds both, and audits both the same way: before touching a single flow, we review what's already in place, what's converting, and what's costing you revenue through neglect.

## What an audit-first approach means for Brisbane businesses

Most agencies start with a proposal. We start with an audit. That means before you sign anything, you get a clear picture of:

- Which flows are missing or underperforming against benchmark
- Where your current platform setup is costing you deliverability or revenue
