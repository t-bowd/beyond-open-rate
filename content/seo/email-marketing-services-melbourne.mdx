## What email marketing services should include

Proper email marketing services go beyond a weekly newsletter. For an ecommerce brand, the core components are abandoned cart and browse abandonment flows, post-purchase and replenishment sequences, win-back campaigns, list segmentation, and deliverability management. Campaigns sit on top of this as promotional layers, but the flows are what generate consistent, compounding revenue month over month.

> **Flow revenue benchmark:** well-built automated flows typically account for 25–35% of total email-attributed revenue, even though they represent a small fraction of total sends.
