Queensland's e-commerce market has grown steadily — outdoor, lifestyle, health and wellness, and specialty retail brands built on Shopify and scaling past the point where a basic email setup still covers everything.

Most of them are on Klaviyo. Few are getting close to what the platform can do.

The gap is almost always the same things: a welcome series and a cart flow built at launch and never revisited, campaigns going to the whole list, and reporting that shows open rate rather than revenue.

## What most Brisbane Klaviyo accounts are missing

**Browse abandonment.** When someone browses a product or category and leaves without adding to cart, that's a clear signal. A well-built browse abandonment flow catches a meaningful percentage of those visitors and brings them back. It's the most consistently underbuilt high-performing flow in e-commerce email.

**Segmentation.** Sending to your full list regardless of engagement is one of the fastest ways to damage deliverability over time. Engagement tiers, purchase behaviour segments, and product category splits make every send more relevant and protect your sender reputation.

**Post-purchase sequences.** The moment after someone buys is the highest-trust point in the customer relationship. A good post-purchase flow includes a review request at the right time, a cross-sell based on what they bought, and a replenishment reminder for consumable products. Most accounts have an order confirmation and nothing else.

**Revenue reporting.** Opens and clicks don't tell you whether the program is working. Revenue per recipient, flow contribution to total revenue, and abandoned cart recovery rate do. We build reporting that gives you that view.

## What we do

**Flow builds and optimisation.** We build or rebuild the core flows — welcome series, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase, win-back — with correct trigger logic, audience exclusions, and tested copy. We tune them month over month based on what the data shows.

**Campaign management.** A monthly calendar of broadcast campaigns: planned, written, designed, and sent to the right segments. We own the cadence so it doesn't fall to whoever has spare time that week.

**Klaviyo migrations.** Full migrations from other platforms — data export and cleaning, segment recreation, flow rebuilding, domain authentication, and a phased warm-up plan.

**Deliverability.** Authentication records configured correctly. List hygiene on a regular cadence. Sunset policies for long-inactive subscribers. Monitoring that catches issues early.
